# Code of conduct

This project-specific policy applies to public collaboration in SENTINEL X issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions. Public participation grants no direct write access or support entitlement.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, not the person's identity or presumed motives.
- Welcome English and French participation; ask for clarification rather than mocking language differences.
- Give actionable reviews, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and credit others' work.
- Respect consent, privacy, source attribution and the owner's authorized project boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate credential exposure, malicious code and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not repost harmful or sensitive material as evidence in an ordinary issue.

## Raising a concern

Contact the owner **@JLV-73 through an already established private channel**. Share only the minimum necessary context, privately. If no such channel exists, wait until one is established rather than publishing personal details. Suspected technical vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The owner may request an edit, pause a discussion, remove harmful content or restrict participation, in proportion to the situation and available repository controls. Concerns are handled as privately as practical, but absolute confidentiality and response deadlines are not promised. Repository access changes remain owner decisions; this document creates no invitations or automatic access changes.

**Français :** respect des personnes, critiques factuelles, confidentialité et consentement. Le harcèlement, la discrimination et la divulgation de données privées sont interdits. Signaler un problème au propriétaire par un canal privé déjà établi, sans publier de données personnelles.
